02 · REA programme

The REA journey

REA (Reduce, Eliminate, Automate) is a bank-wide initiative designed to challenge how we work, eliminate inefficiencies, and unlock new ways of delivering value.

Launched in February 2026, BPM created a platform for every staff member to contribute ideas through the Innovation Nexus. Since then, the programme has rapidly grown into a powerful engine for innovation — turning ideas into real solutions.

  • Driving automation across key business areas
  • Enabling real-time visibility of operations
  • Reducing manual work and operational risk
  • Delivering faster, smarter customer journeys
From ideas to outcomes — REA is driving real impact across NCBA.
